# Raw report: candidates for "Astronomy and astrology" and "Magic and divination" (25.10.2026)

One research agent (Claude Opus 5.5) mined the earlier sweeps (02_candidates.md, raw/wave1_*, raw/wave2_*, ru_series_crosscheck.json, the thematic files) and then checked about 15 obvious candidates on the web. Its report is kept here as delivered; the dossier built from it is [../astronomy-magic.md](../astronomy-magic.md). Statuses marked "from memory" or "low" were not verified.

## Corrections to earlier files

- **Hipparchus, our #1 candidate, now has an English translation.** 02_candidates.md recorded EN as «нет», but Glover published *The Astronomy of Hipparchus* with CUP in 2026 (DOI 10.1017/9781009607544). The Laestrygonians study already cites it (article.md n. 17; dossier.md). Schironi's Routledge edition with translation is also announced. Hipparchus is now a Russian-first project.
- **The Aratus scholia and Achilles' *Isagoge* may already be in English.** Schironi's Aratus Project at Michigan (NSF-funded, 2019–23) claims a complete English translation of the scholia, Achilles, Leontius and the Catasterismi on an open platform (https://classicalstudies.org/aratus-project-ancient-scholarship-and-astronomy-multimodal-platform). The platform could not be found live. Check before announcing either text.

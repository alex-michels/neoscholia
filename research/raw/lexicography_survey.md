# Raw report: candidates for the "Lexicography" section (26.10.2026)

One research agent (Claude Opus 5.5) mined the earlier sweeps (02_candidates.md, music.md, raw/wave1_*, raw/wave2_*, ru_series_crosscheck.json) and checked about 15 candidates on the web. Its report is kept here as delivered; the dossier built from it is [../lexicography.md](../lexicography.md). "J" = judges' average (scholarly / readers / feasibility) from wave2_ranking.json; "IA" = archive.org identifier.

## Corrections to our files

- **Pollux IV in Russian.** E. V. Gertsman's own bibliography (artcenter.ru, 2024) lists «Инструментальный каталог Поллукса» (Л., 1988, pp. 7–29) and «Уцелевшее письменное наследие античного инструментоведения» (СПб.: РГПУ, 2020, 408 pp.), which probably translate Pollux's instrument passages. Neither is online. The same bibliography does not list «Древнеримский „Ономастикон“» (Квадривиум 2022) cited in music.md; that citation is probably wrong.
- **Italian versions we missed.** Pollux IX.94–129 (games): open-access edition with commentary, Costanza 2019 (CC BY; Greek confirmed, Italian translation not confirmed). Zenobius: Italian edition, translation and commentary of Book I of the Athos recension (Ruta 2020).

## Top 5

1. Zenobius, Century I. 2. Pollux (the English window lasts until Feb 2027). 3. Ps.-Ammonius, letter Α. 4. Apollonius Sophista (companion to the Homer scholia; a new edition is coming). 5. Nonius, Book V. Close reserves: Phrynichus, Erotian.

First publication suggested: Pollux IX.94–129 (dice, knucklebones, ball games, children's games; ~3k words): Russian apparently free (not searched specifically), English free until Olson; Costanza's open Italian edition as a cross-check; fasc. 2 US PD from 1.1.2027; IX.106 cites Nausicaa's ball game (Od. 8). Then Zenobius Century I as the section's main series. Pollux IV.57–90 (music) after Gertsman 1988/2020 has been seen; if he translated it, publish English-first with a new annotated Russian version crediting him.

## Summary for the reviewer

Both translations were drafted directly from the supplied Greek and revised against all 67 units and the supplied apparatus. The hardest issue is the coexistence of additive interval sizes with the rational tone9:8. Review U018 first, because its eight-and-a-half and eight-and-a-quarter endpoints do not yield equal musical divisions. U039 then combines incompatible models and gives a positive residual where the rational comparison requires either a deficiency or zero. The repeated double-fourth and double-fifth labels have been translated literally, while their likely octave-compound meanings and every preferred correction are separated in doubts and text issues. The fifteen-note system has been counted and reconstructed, including the editorially supplied seventh degree and the shared octave boundary. The square-number examples work only for division into two integer squares, a restriction missing from the printed wording. The P-only expansions retain their exact double-bracket boundaries, and their conjectural readings are not presented as manuscript certainty. The least secure semantic decisions are τὸ πᾶν in U004, the temporal contrast in U037, and the unresolved †ἀμφότερον† in U050. The ellipsis in U058 is made visible by bracketed supply of the whole tetrachord as subject. Checked lexical evidence comes from LSJ entries, while checked doctrinal parallels come chiefly from Cleonides and the Sectio canonis; references not actually inspected remain explicitly marked from memory. No independent lexical attestation was established for αὐτοσυμμετρία or ἡμιεπόγδοος, and no Platonic locus is asserted as the certain source of U064. The contamination record discloses incidental bare references to a prohibited later author in automatically displayed editorial notes, none of which was used. A reviewer should prioritize the numerical-model conflicts and the corrupt P reading before revisiting the more routine choices of style.

## check_composed.py on `final`

`python -I scripts/check_composed.py <ws-cmp> final`: 28 units checked, 2 problems.
- `missing units: U029–U067`: expected.
- `U020 RU ratio «сверхосминного» without [n:m] right after it`: a false positive. The checker's ratio regex matches
  «сверхосминного» inside «полусверхосминного», so its exemption for words beginning «полусверх-» never applies.
  DECISIONS (ratio table and L03) prescribe «полусверхосминного» without a bracket, so the text stays; the exemption in the
  checker should test the two characters before the match.

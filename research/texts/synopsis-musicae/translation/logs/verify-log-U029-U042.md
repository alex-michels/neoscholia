## Checked and left as composed

- **U034, «октава — как достигающая полноты через восемь».** I agree with the composer's departure from the DECISIONS sample («как завершаемая через восемь»). With the sample, τελούμενον and τελειούμενον would get the same verb, which is the error listed for C and F. It would also hide the H Par. V reading that the edition adopts against Bas. The open question to the editor stays.
- **U029, ταύτῃ τοι «Потому-то» / 'That is why'.** The causal reading fits the P context, since U028 gives the reason (continuity in matter). The composer's open question stays.
- **Greek in the running text.** Only U033 and U035, as DECISIONS § 2 requires. The signs ⟨⟨ (U042) and ⟩⟩ (U032) match the Greek.

# Xylander: revision log (passes 5–6)

Revised file: `xylander-revised.json` (same format as the draft; 58 segments, ids and order checked against the Latin). The changes were applied to the draft by the script `revise_xyl.py`, so every difference from the draft is listed here. 40 segments were touched (text, notes or doubts); 18 are unchanged.

Sources opened in the revision **[checked]**: Lewis & Short, Gaffiot, DMLBS, Du Cange through Logeion (clavus, clavis, diversus, respectus, miraculum, tensio, amplitudo, continuus, succedo, coaptatio, obliquus); Boethius, *De institutione musica*, ed. Friedlein, books 1–2, in the Latin Wikisource transcription of that edition (text of 1.2, 1.8, 1.10, 1.21, 1.22; chapter headings of 1.16–1.20, 2.25–2.27, 2.31); page image of p. 45; the diplomatic lines (`printed`) of X15, X23, X24, X34, X38. Not verified, still **[from memory]**: Boethius 3.1–3 and 5.9 (the Wikisource pages for books 3 and 5 do not carry Boethius' text), Cleonides, Nicomachus, Theon, Euclid, Ptolemy, Aristoxenus, Aristides Quintilianus, Plato. No forbidden material was met; contamination stays empty.

- All numbers and ratios recomputed (X15–16, X18–20, X23–24, X26, X29–30, X32, X34–36, X38, X42, X47, X49–51): the draft's checks are correct, including the cents values and the remark that the four pairs in X32 are all plain octaves.

- Text issues X18, X23, X24, X32 stand; X34 corrected; X38 added.

## F. For the merger

1. The comma of X34 and *qua* in X38 are matters for the data file.
2. *Modulatio* (X44) and the pair "paraphonon / antiphonon" (Latin *paraphonum, antiphonum*, left in Greek-style transliteration in English) should be settled together with the Vinet version.
3. *Amplitudo cavitatis* (X41): "size" and "width" are both defensible.

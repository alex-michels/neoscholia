# Check of composed group 4 (Vinet V34–V66)

Checked `work/composed-g4.json` against `packet-g4.json` and `common.json`, one segment at a time. For each segment I checked:

- the Latin against the running text (nothing omitted or added, the construal, every number);
- the ratio brackets and every `[ ]`;
- the general and place cards and the agreed points;
- the corroborated error (V42) and the recheck items R14 (V43), R15 (V46) and R16 (V64);
- terms against about.md and within the group;
- the notes and their sources;
- the anchors.

The file as it stood before the check is kept at `work/chk4/composed-g4.before-check.json`.

**Result.** 11 segments changed. All the changes are in notes, sources or decision lists; no running text needed correction. The anchors are verbatim (55 notes, re-verified after the edits), and every ratio name in the running text has its bracket.

## Changes

1. **V34, decisions.** Added G47, which `general_cards_listing_this_segment` lists for V34. It is applied in substance: non-composita «несоставные», with the note keeping it apart from simplicia. Bookkeeping only.
2. **V35, note 1, source.** "Lewis & Short s.v. tamquam [from memory]" changed to "tamquam I ‘as, just as, as it were’ [checked]". Verified in L&S online.
3. **V37, note 1, source.** "Xylander X32 … [from memory: card G26]" changed to "[checked]". X32 (inaequali tempore … simul eodem tempore) was verified in packet-g2.
4. **V40, note.**
   - The claim "for the multiple ratios Vinet uses the forms in -plex" was too broad. In V44 he writes quincuplex, which fits, but also duplum sesquialterum. The note now says so, in RU and EN.
   - The X36 source (super bipartiens tertias) was verified in packet-g2 and is now marked "[checked]", not "[from memory: card G28]".
5. **V44, note 2, source.** "[checked: card V07]" changed to "[checked]". X38 (ad excessum quantitatis respiciendo) was verified in packet-g2.
6. **V52.**
   - Note 1, source: the X44 Latin and its rendering in composed-g2 («мелодическое движение» / "melodic movement") were verified, so the source is now [checked], not [from memory].
   - Note 2: «энгармоническим» changed to «энармоническим». The group was inconsistent: the notes to V59 and V62 have «энармонический». «Энармоника, энармонический род» is also the usual Russian term for the Greek genus; «энгармонизм» is the modern enharmonic equivalence.
   - Note 2: the source line cited Theon p. 55.8, but the text did not use it. Added "у Феона Смирнского γένος ἐναρμόνιον" / "Theon of Smyrna: γένος ἐναρμόνιον", which is what C cited it for.
7. **V55, note 1, source.** "Vinet V04, V54 … [from memory: card V09]" changed to "[checked]". V04 quinimo (after an affirmation; group 3 renders «более того») was verified in packet-g3 and composed-g3.
8. **V57, note, rewritten.**
   - The old note said that simplex "means ‘incomposite’" and in the next sentence that Vinet "keeps this word apart from non-compositum". Read together, these contradict each other. The note now separates sense and word: by sense «несоставной»; but it is not the word non-compositum of V34, hence «простой» (G47).
   - The old note cited "X49, X51" for coalitus. X51 is the parallel of V59 (the ditone), not V57; the reference is now X49 only.
   - Added a check of the sense: Xylander at X49 writes trisemitonium incompositum, quoted in the group-2 rendering («путём слияния … несоставной трёхполутон» / "by coalescence … the incomposite trihemitone"). This is the Latin parallel for the gloss.
   - Source: X49 now [checked] instead of [from memory: card G19].
9. **V58, note 2, source.** "Xylander X50 … [from memory: card G35]" changed to "[checked]". "ad movendos affectus et luctum ciendum efficacius" was verified.
10. **V59, note 2.**
    - The old note stated as fact that the subjunctive in quod sit optimum gives the namers' reason. A&G § 540 is the classical rule. Vinet is a Renaissance author, and sol rates the distancing nuance as only possible.
    - The note now says «по классической норме …» / "by the classical rule …". The translation («на том основании, что он наилучший») is unchanged.
11. **V65, note 2 (tactus), rewritten.**
    - The old note argued «Tactus в XVI в. значит и „такт", но Вине прямо связывает касания и удары с рукой». That argument does not separate the two senses: the sixteenth-century tactus, the beat, was itself a motion of the hand. Ornithoparchus (1517, Dowland's translation, 1609): "a certaine motion, made by the hand of the chiefe singer … which directs a Song according to measure".
    - The note now gives the actual grounds for «касания»: the plural, variis, and the pairing with pulsus. It keeps the agreed statement that the text does not say "beat".
    - Source: "agreed point on tactus (claude, astra) [from memory]" replaced by Ornithoparchus/Dowland [checked] and R. DeFord, *Tactus, Mensuration, and Rhythm in Renaissance Music* (2015) [checked: scope].

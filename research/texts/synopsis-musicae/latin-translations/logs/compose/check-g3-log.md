## Changes

### V22, note on «как бы восемь с половиной» / 'as it were eight and a half' (last sentence, ru + en + source)
- **Was:** «Следующая фраза (V23) объясняет, что точное деление возможно только геометрически.» / "The next clause (V23) explains that an exact division is possible only geometrically."
- **Now:** «Следующая фраза (V23) обосновывает эти дробные числа: единицу, которую нельзя разделить числами, можно разделить геометрически, когда она рассматривается в материи.» / "The next clause (V23) gives the reason for these fractional numbers: the unit, which cannot be divided by numbers, can be divided geometrically when it is considered in matter."
- Source: "Latin of V23 [checked]" added.
- **Reason:**
  - V23 is attached by quandoquidem. It gives the reason for octo cum semisse / octo cum quadrante: the unit (the excess of 9 over 8) is indivisible in numbers but divisible in matter.
  - V23 says nothing of an "exact" division, nor that division is possible "only" geometrically.
  - The old sentence contradicted the same note, which calls Vinet's division arithmetical (equal increments of length, hence veluti).
  - It also attributed an exact bisection to this passage, against the agreed point on V24/V30 and the G45 evidence ("не приписывать им построение среднего пропорционального").

### V15, note on «ни протяжённости, как точка в геометрии» (ru + en + source)
- **Was:** «Dimensio — букв. «измерение, протяжение».» / "Dimensio is literally 'measurement, extension'."
- **Now:** «Dimensio — «измерение», в позднем употреблении также «протяжённость, размеры».» / "Dimensio is 'measuring, measurement', in later use also 'extent, dimensions'."
- Source: "Lewis & Short s.v. dimensio I, II.2 [checked]" added.
- **Reason:**
  - L&S gives "a measuring" as the proper sense. "Extent, dimensions" is a transferred, late sense (II.2: Macrobius, Ammianus), so it is not the literal one.
  - The dictionary claim had no source.
- The text keeps «протяжённость» / dimension (G43).

### V16, note on «нестройный и неблагозвучный» (source only)
- **Was:** "Lewis & Short s.v. absurdus I and s.v. absonus I [checked: translators A, C]; card G44"
- **Now:** "Cicero, De oratore 3.41 ('quasi extra modum absona atque absurda'), The Latin Library [checked]; Lewis & Short s.v. absurdus I (cited as vox absona et absurda) [checked] and s.v. absonus I [checked: translators A, C]; card G44"
- **Reason:** The note quotes "vox absona atque absurda". That wording is Cicero's own (De or. 3.41: "mollis vox aut muliebris aut quasi extra modum absona atque absurda"). L&S, the only source given, prints it as "vox absona et absurda". The source now covers the wording quoted. The note text is unchanged.

### V08, note on «упорядочивает» / 'orders' (source only)
- **Was:** "Lewis & Short s.v. compono [from memory]"
- **Now:** "Lewis & Short s.v. compono II.C.1 ('to arrange, adjust, order, set in order') [checked]"
- **Reason:** I checked the sense the note relies on (G41: componere in V08 = «упорядочивать»), and it is in the dictionary.

1. Base text and reference grid. The base is the Basel edition of 1556 (Bas.), and every place is cited by Bas. page and line. A marker |page.line| stands immediately before the first letter of each Bas. line, with no space after it, and inside a word where Bas. divides one. All 257 lines from 23.1 to 33.21 carry exactly one marker each, in order. A section that ends inside a Bas. line carries that line's marker, and the next section begins without one. Sections 2, 4 and 6 begin a new paragraph; sections 3, 5 and 7 continue the paragraph before them.

2. Bas. corrected by the oldest manuscript. Bas. is kept unless its reading is a misprint, or another witness gives a better or the original text. Each place is weighed by sense, grammar, the author's usage, harmonic theory and the agreement of the witnesses. The text departs from Bas. at 35 places:
- 3 are passages found only in P, printed in ⟨⟨ ⟩⟩.
- 2 are supplements from Xylander's margin that no witness has: ⟨διάτονος μέσων⟩ at 25.7 and ⟨διὰ⟩ at 31.1.
- 18 are places where Bas. stands alone against H Par. V. Three of these are plain misprints (περιοχή, διεζευγμένη, ὑμέναιος). The others include the order of the heading, μετὰ, γὰρ at 24.23, τῇ, λαμβάνοντες, τελειούμενον, ἐπαναδιπλουμένων, πως, the omitted δέ at 29.13, δὶς at 29.15, ἢ at 29.24, τοὺς πρὸ, ἐμπεριέχον, ἐντιθεμένων, and the clause μεταφορᾷ … τούτων at 33.14, lost in Bas.
- 1 is a place where Bas. agrees with V against H Par. (ἔχον, 24.7).
- 6 are places where Bas. agrees with Par., the printed tradition, against H V: ἔχων 25.22, ὅσον, δὶς 29.14, τὸν ἕκτον, διάστασιν, σχολαιοτέραις.
- 4 are places where Bas. Par. V share an error and H has the right text: ὅλην, ἡ at 31.23 and τοῦ at 32.23, all three confirmed by Xylander's margin, and γοερώτερον.
- 1 follows P against all four other witnesses: συστημάτων at 30.23, on the author's own terminology.

3. The weight of H. H, the oldest witness, weighs most but is not followed by default. When H stands alone and sense or the author's usage is against it, its reading is recorded and rejected: μόν(ος) at 25.7, μοῖραν, γάρ for δέ at 28.17 and 32.21, οὐ γάρ at 32.1, ἐξάρηνται and γενομένης. H's isolated γάρ for δέ is treated alike at all three places, as a confusion of compendia. Where H, Par. and V all agree on γάρ (24.23), it is adopted.

4. The author's usage is not classicised. Byzantine and non-classical forms and terms are kept wherever they are unanimous or best attested:
- ὀνομάσοι with ἄν;
- the nominative ad sensum λαμβάνοντες;
- ἑξκαιδέκατος;
- ἑκκαιδεκάχορδον, the author's own count, explained by τὸ ἐν πεντεκαιδεκαχόρδῳ ἑκκαιδεκάχορδον at 25.11–12;
- the note names ὑπάτη διάτονος and παράμεσος, the adjectival τρίτη / διάτονος / νήτη διεζευγμένη, and μέση μόνως;
- the generic διπλασιεπιμερής for 8:3, and πενταπλασίων;
- δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων and δὶς διὰ πέντε for the fourth and the fifth above the octave (8:3 and 3:1), consistently in sections 3, 4 and 5.
Xylander's marginal proposals that classicise these names or add words that are not needed are recorded and rejected: the deletion of δίς at 29.8–9, διὰ πασῶν καὶ … at 28.11, and τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς at 25.24–25. Morphology is kept; spelling is normalised (see 5).

5. Orthography and punctuation. The text follows standard modern editorial practice:
- Accents, breathings and iota subscript are normalised.
- Word division is normalised: διὰ πασῶν, τό τε, ἐπεί τοί γε, κἀκεῖθεν, ὀκτὼ τέταρτον.
- Spelling slips that leave the word unchanged are normalised: ἔνατος, ὀξέος for the unanimous ὀξέως, κολλάβων for κολάβων, ἄρρευστον.
- Elision is marked with ’, and the Greek is in Unicode NFC.
- Xylander's reference signs * and † are not reproduced.
Punctuation is for sense: comma, high point (·) and full stop. Parentheses are used only where Bas. has them (26.18–21), and there are no dashes. Capitals are used only at paragraph beginnings, in the heading and the subscription, and for Πλάτων. A paragraph is separated by a blank line. Paragraphs begin where Bas. marks a break, or where a new subject starts: 23.3, 24.15, 24.19, 25.2, 25.19, 27.9, 27.14 (Παρὰ), 28.8, 28.13, 28.25, 29.11, 29.23, 30.23 (Τῶν δὲ συστημάτων), 31.10 and 33.21. A P passage that opens with a formula announcing a new remark (Σκοπητέον, Ἰστέον) is set as its own paragraph; the P passage that continues an argument (εἰ γάρ, after 27.14) runs on.

6. Critical signs.
- ⟨ ⟩ marks words added without any witness: Xylander's διάτονος μέσων and διὰ, and Vincent's ἡ in P's fr. 2.
- ⟨⟨ ⟩⟩ marks P's three passages absent from Bas. Par. H V (after 27.14, 29.23 and 30.23). They are printed as P's text and not claimed for the text of 1008.
- †…† is used once, inside a P passage (ἀμφότερον).
- { } and *** are not used. A possible lacuna after τριπλάσιος (29.16) is discussed in a doubt.
Where P has the same place in another form, that form is a variant in the apparatus and is never printed as well (Περὶ, Σκοπητέον δὲ ὡς, P's wind-instrument clause).

7. P. P (BnF gr. 2448) is known only through Vincent, so its passages are printed from Vincent's text, with the manuscript readings taken from his footnotes. Within P passages:
- P's text is kept wherever it gives sense: κεκρυμμένων, ὅτι … τοῦτο, ἐργάζεται, οἱ τῶν ἐν μέσῳ, συγκρινομένων, μέλος.
- Vincent's corrections are adopted where P is ungrammatical or arithmetically impossible: τὸ τῶν ἀριθμῶν, τῆς, τέμνειν, τὴν, διαστημάτων, μονάδων.
- P is corrected by conjecture where it garbles a technical term or number that the common text itself attests: σύμφθαρσις (twice) and ἐκ συμφθάρσεως (twice), after 27.10, 32.10–11 and 32.17; προσλαμβανόμενον, after 25.4–5; ἑξκαιδέκατον, after 26.16 and 29.20.

8. Apparatus. The apparatus is positive, with one entry per place of variation, in text order.
- Form: 'lemma witnesses] variant witnesses | variant witnesses', using om., add. and del.
- Sigla always stand in the order Bas. H Par. V P, followed by:
  - Xyl. (Xylander's margin; the siglum itself means the margin, and his wording is quoted in the note);
  - Vinc. (a correction printed in Vincent's text);
  - Vinc. in nota (a suggestion in his footnote, with (dub.) if he is tentative);
  - coni. (a conjecture of this edition; the doubts say whose proposal it was).
- Every witness extant at the place is cited.
- Readings of H and V are given in normalised spelling. Diplomatic forms are kept only for non-words or doubtful expansions (ἐξά̣ρηνται, μόν(ος)).
- Orthographic differences are summarised per witness in the orthographica. These are itacism, accent, breathing, movable nu, elision, iota subscript or adscript, and word division. They also include ο/ω where the form is unchanged.
- Two kinds of small difference do go into the apparatus:
  - ο/ω in a participle that changes its gender (ἔχον/ἔχων, ἐμπεριέχων);
  - an accent difference that yields another word on which witnesses agree (βάρει H Par. at 28.18).
- An accent slip of Bas. alone that leaves the word the same goes to the orthographica (μύρια).

9. Departures, doubts and alternatives.
- Departures list every substantive departure from Bas., the three P passages included. Conjectures inside P passages are given in the apparatus and the doubts, since Bas. has no text there.
- Doubts keep Bas. (in P passages, P's text) and set out the options. Where the arbiter decided a disputed place for a minority witness or a conjecture, the doubt is kept as a record for the human editor (30.23, P 340.9–10, P 340.13–18).
- Alternatives for translation list readings that are rejected but make sense, especially those of the printed editions, which earlier translations follow. One rendering is advised for δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων / δὶς διὰ πέντε throughout: no multiplier, with a note giving 'octave plus fourth / fifth'.

### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 23.2: ΗΚΡΙΒΩΜΕΝΗ ΣΥΝΟΨΙΣ (ἠκριβωμένη σύνοψις) → σύνοψις ἠκριβωμένη (H Par. V). Word order of the heading. Bas. stands alone against all three witnesses, including Par., the reprint of the editio princeps. The 1556 order is most likely a typographical rearrangement for the two-line heading in capitals. The sense is the same.
- Bas. 23.8: κατὰ → μετὰ (H Par. V). κατά with the genitive συμμετρίας τῆς προσηκούσης gives no sense here: 'according to' would need the accusative. μετὰ συμμετρίας … καὶ ἀναλογίας picks up συμμετρίας ἄτερ καὶ ἀναλογίας (23.5–6). Bas. stands alone against H Par. V.
- Bas. 24.7: ἔχων → ἔχον (H Par.). This is the predicative participle of διαφαίνεται. Its subject is τὸ πᾶν συναρμολογούμενον καὶ συμβιβαζόμενον (24.5–6), so the neuter is required and the masculine is ungrammatical. V agrees with Bas., probably by an independent ο/ω slip; Bas. has the reverse error ἔχον for ἔχων at 25.22.
- Bas. 24.18: περωχὴ → περιοχή (H Par. V). Misprint in Bas. (π + ερ-ligature + ωχή: ι is omitted and ω is printed for ο), which gives a non-word. All three witnesses have περιοχή.
- Bas. 24.23: δὲ (printed δ’ according to Claude's Bas. note and both Claude collators) → γὰρ (H Par. V). Bas. stands alone against H Par. V. The γάρ clause explains why a note that could not be fitted to another is not ἐναρμόνιος (24.20–23). The elided δ’ before a consonant reported for Bas. is anomalous and points to a misprint, possibly a misreading of a γάρ compendium in the printer's copy (Par. prints γάρ here with a κ-like compendium sort). δέ would also make sense: see alternatives.

### Сомнения

- Bas. 24.5: The section file contradicts itself about what Bas. prints. The decided diplomatic line reads ὀνομάσοι. Claude's Bas. note on 24.5 ('accent (resolved)') gives the print as ὀνομάζει, 'confirmed', and the V collator's note also takes ὀνομάζει as the Bas. lemma. The constituted text is ὀνομάσοι either way: H Par. V have it, and τάχα δ’ ἄν τις requires the optative. Only the apparatus and the departures are affected. The decided line is followed, so there is no entry. Варианты: (a) Bas. = ὀνομάσοι (decided line): no variant, no entry. (b) Bas. = ὀνομάζει: add the entry 'ὀνομάσοι H Par. V] ὀνομάζει Bas.' and a departure, because the indicative with ἄν is impossible here.
- Bas. 24.23: The section file again contradicts itself. The decided diplomatic line reads εἰ δὲ μὴ; Claude's Bas. note records the print as εἰ δ’ μὴ, with an elided δ’ before a consonant and a clear elision mark, and both Claude collators give the Bas. lemma as εἰ δ’ μὴ (the Par. collator: 'Bas. δ’ checked on the image'). Варианты: (a) δὲ as in the decided line; (b) δ’ as in the Bas. note and the collators' notes.

### Чтения для переводчика

- Bas. 24.23: в тексте «εἰ γὰρ μὴ οὕτως ἐκφωνηθείη», иначе «εἰ δὲ μὴ οὕτως ἐκφωνηθείη» (Bas. (γὰρ H Par. V)). δέ is also good Greek and good sense ('but if it is not so uttered…'): an adversative continuation instead of the explanatory 'for'. It is rejected because Bas. stands alone and its δ’ before a consonant looks like a misprint. Translations made from Bas. will have read δέ.
- Bas. 24.1–3: в тексте «ἡ δὲ μουσικὴ αὐτοσυμμετρία τίς ἐστι καὶ ἀναλογία, τὸ πᾶν, οἷα τοῦ παντὸς ἁρμονία τυγχάνουσα (Bas. punctuation: τὸ πᾶν in apposition, 'music is a kind of self-symmetry and proportion, the All, as being the harmony of the All')», иначе «(a) …καὶ ἀναλογία τὸ πᾶν, οἷα… : τὸ πᾶν taken adverbially, 'a self-symmetry and proportion through and through'. Par. points this way (ἀναλογία, τὸ πᾶν. οἷα). (b) …καὶ ἀναλογία· τὸ πᾶν οἷα τοῦ παντὸς ἁρμονία τυγχάνουσα : H and V punctuate after ἀναλογία and not after τὸ πᾶν, which joins τὸ πᾶν to the participial phrase ('being wholly, as it were, the harmony of the All').» (punctuation only: Par. for (a); H V for (b)). There is no variant in the wording, but the construction of τὸ πᾶν is open. The apposition picks up συνέχειν τὸ πᾶν (23.3–4) and τοῦ παντὸς (24.2, 24.5), and the οἷα clause then justifies the bold equation of music with the All; the adverbial reading is equally possible. The translator should choose knowingly.

- Bas. 25.2: τῇ H Par. V] τῷ Bas. — H writes τῆι with iota adscript. V τῇ in the established line; the collators' note reads τῆ without iota.
- Bas. 25.4: λαμβάνοντες H Par. V] λαμβάνοντος Bas. — In H (64v.17), above -νον of λαμβάνον|τες, there is an interlinear υ with a tick and a ʒ-shaped sign, in the scribe's ink and a smaller module. It may be a correction or variant such as λαμβάνου(σι), but its reading is unclear and it is not entered as a reading (see doubts). Claude's Bas. note proposed λαμβανόντων as a conjecture; no witness has it.
- Bas. 25.7: ⟨διάτονος μέσων⟩ Xyl.] om. Bas. H Par. V — The supplement is required by the 15-note count of 25.11-17; the loss is a saut du même au même (μέσων … μέσων). Cf. Cleonides, Isag. 4: λιχανὸς μέσων διάτονος; Gaudentius, Harm. intr. 6: λιχανὸς τῶν μέσων ἤτοι διάτονος μέσων. Xylander's margin: «* διάτονος μέσων addo».
- Bas. 25.7: μόνως Bas. Par. V] μόν(ος) H (suspension, expansion doubtful) — At the line end (64v.20) H has μόν with a curl over ν. The established transcription expands it μόν(ος) and marks it doubtful; the H notes also consider μόν(η) and μόν(ον), and a compendium for μόν(ως) cannot be excluded. The entry is recorded because μόνος/μόνη would be an adjective with μέση rather than the adverb.
- Bas. 25.8-9: διεζευγμένη (post διάτονος) H Par. V] διαζευγμένη Bas. — Bas. prints the δια- compendium. Bas. itself has διεζευγμένη in 25.8 and 25.9.
- Bas. 25.22: ἔχων H V] ἔχον Bas. Par. — The masculine agrees with ὁ τόνος … διιστάμενος. At 25.20 (τὸ ἡμιτόνιον … ἔχον) all witnesses agree.
- Bas. 25.24: ὅσον H V] οἷον Bas. Par. — It is parallel to ὅσον in 25.25, which all witnesses have.
- Bas. 25.24: ὀκτὼ (prius) Bas. H Par. V] τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς ὀκτὼ Xyl. — With the supplement the sense becomes 'half of the ratio of 9 to 8'. The transmitted text means 8½, a point halfway between 8 and 9, and 26.1-2 (the unit cannot be divided in numbers, but can be divided in matter) justifies exactly this halving of the unit 9 − 8. So the supplement is unnecessary. Xylander's margin: «* τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς addo».
- Bas. 25.25: ὀκτὼ (alterum) Bas. H Par. V] τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς ὀκτὼ Xyl. — Same reasoning as the previous entry: 8¼. Xylander's margin: «† iterum eadem verba addo». Bas. Par. write ὀκτωτέταρτον as one word and H V write ὀκτὼ τέταρτον, which is word division only.
- Bas. 26.4: νευρὰν Bas. Par. V] μοῖραν H — H (65r.21) writes μο̣ι̣ρὰν: ο and ι are uncertain (underdotted), and the second letter could also be read as an open ω. The word is accented on the last syllable, which fits νευράν and not μοῖραν (properly accented on the first syllable). This suggests a slip in H or its exemplar (ν/μ and a misread ευ-ligature). P fr. 1 (a parallel, not a witness) also uses νευρὰς for strings. See alternatives.

### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 25.2: τῷ → τῇ (H Par. V). The article goes with the feminine προόδῳ καὶ τάξει (25.3): τῇ ἀπὸ τοῦ βαρέος ἐπὶ τὸ ὀξὺ προόδῳ. Bas. is the only witness with the masculine/neuter form, and nothing in the sentence can take it.
- Bas. 25.4: λαμβάνοντος → λαμβάνοντες (H Par. V). A nominative plural participle ad sensum (sc. φθόγγοι): 'the notes, which take their names from the progression and order from low to high'. Bas.'s genitive singular agrees with no word in the sentence, and Bas. stands alone against all three witnesses. A loose nominative of this kind is common in Byzantine prose and should not be normalised to λαμβανόντων (the conjecture in Claude's Bas. notes).
- Bas. 25.7: παρυπάτη μέσων, * μέση μόνως (the supplement stands only in Xylander's margin: «* διάτονος μέσων addo») → παρυπάτη μέσων, ⟨διάτονος μέσων,⟩ μέση μόνως (Xyl. (marginal supplement), a conjecture with no witness, so printed in ⟨ ⟩). The list in all witnesses has only 14 names: μέση is the 7th and νήτη ὑπερβολαίων the 14th. The text itself needs 15: 25.11-12 says the names complete τὸ ἐν πεντεκαιδεκαχόρδῳ ἑκκαιδεκάχορδον, and 25.14-17 says the 1st note is to the 8th as the 8th to the 15th, which is the double octave of the Greater Perfect System, proslambanomenos–mese–nete hyperbolaion. Mese must therefore be the 8th note, and the missing name is the diatonic lichanos meson (Cleonides, Isag. 4: λιχανὸς μέσων διάτονος). Xylander's form διάτονος μέσων follows the author's own pattern διάτονος διεζευγμένη / διάτονος ὑπερβολαίων, and is the very form of Gaudentius, Harm. intr. 6 (λιχανὸς τῶν μέσων ἤτοι διάτονος μέσων; also διάτονος ὑπάτων). His asterisk places it where the gap is, and the loss is a simple saut du même au même from μέσων to μέσων (παρυπάτη μέσων, διάτονος μέσων, μέση). The omission is shared by all witnesses, so it goes back at least to their common source, perhaps even to the author's own slip. The editor may prefer to leave the list as transmitted and note the gap; the supplement is printed because the passage's own count and its argument (25.11-17) require it. On the wording see doubts.
- Bas. 25.8-9: διαζευγμέ|νη (δια- compendium) → διεζευγμένη (H Par. V). A misprint in Bas.: the δια- compendium was set for διε-. Bas. itself prints διεζευγμένη twice in the same list (25.8 τρίτη διεζευγμένη, 25.9 νήτη διεζευγμένη). A perfect participle without the ε- of the perfect (διαζευγμένη) has no support.
- Bas. 25.22: ἔχον → ἔχων (H V (against Bas. Par.)). The subject is ὁ τόνος, and the participle stands beside the masculine διιστάμενος (25.22-23), so it must be masculine. Bas. Par. ἔχον either repeats ἔχον from 25.20 (said there of τὸ ἡμιτόνιον) or is an ο/ω slip. The oldest witness, H, agrees with V.
- Bas. 25.24: οἷον * ὀκτὼ → ὅσον ὀκτὼ (H V (against Bas. Par.)). The two clauses are parallel: ὡς εἶναι τὸ ἡμιτόνιον ὅσον ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ, τὴν δὲ δίεσιν ὅσον ὀκτὼ τέταρτον, where the second ὅσον is in all witnesses. ὅσον expresses a quantity ('as much as 8½ / 8¼'), which is what the passage is about. H, the oldest witness, and V agree; Bas. and Par. agree here as in another error (ἔχον 25.22) and in ὀκτωτέταρτον (25.25), and probably both go back to the Venice princeps of 1532, which Par. reprints. οἷον also makes sense (see alternatives).

### Сомнения

- Bas. 25.4 (H 64v.17): An interlinear addition in H above λαμβάνον|τες: a small υ with a tick and a ʒ-shaped sign, in the scribe's ink. Варианты: (a) λαμβάνοντες, H's line text with Par. V (adopted); (b) λαμβάνου(σι), if the interlinear is a correction or variant, giving a finite verb; (c) λαμβανόντων, Claude's Bas. note (conjecture, agreeing with φθόγγων). The H notes also list λαμβανούσ(ας), which gives no construction.
- Bas. 25.6: All witnesses have ὑπάτη διάτονος for the 4th note of the system, whose standard name is λιχανὸς ὑπάτων διάτονος (Cleonides, Isag. 4) or διάτονος ὑπάτων (Gaudentius, Harm. intr. 6); the qualifier is regularly the genitive plural of the tetrachord. Варианты: (a) keep ὑπάτη διάτονος (adopted) as the author's naming, 'the diatonic hypate'; (b) emend to ὑπάτων διάτονος (conjecture, Claude's Bas. note), assuming an early misreading of a suspended -ων among the surrounding ὑπάτη; (c) διάτονος ὑπάτων (conjecture), the author's own order for the other tetrachords (διάτονος διεζευγμένη, διάτονος ὑπερβολαίων) and Gaudentius' form, but it needs a transposition as well.
- Bas. 25.7 (wording of the supplement): That a 7th name (the diatonic lichanos meson) is missing is certain; its wording is not, since no witness has it. Варианты: (a) διάτονος μέσων (Xyl., adopted); (b) μέσων διάτονος or λιχανὸς μέσων διάτονος (the standard forms, cf. Cleonides, Isag. 4); (c) μέση διάτονος, by analogy with the transmitted ὑπάτη διάτονος for the 4th note.
- Bas. 25.7: μέση μόνως (Bas. Par. V) against H's suspended μόν(ος) (expansion doubtful). Варианты: (a) μόνως, adverb (adopted): 'just mese', the name with no tetrachord qualifier, after a run of names in …μέσων; (b) μόνος / μόνη, adjective: 'mese alone, single', possibly meaning that the mese is counted once as the end of the first octachord and the start of the second (25.12-14).
- Bas. 26.4: νευρὰν (Bas. Par. V) against μοῖραν (written μοιρὰν) in H, the oldest witness. Варианты: (a) νευρὰν, 'string' (adopted); (b) μοῖραν, 'portion, segment' (H).
- Bas. 26.8: The established Bas. line reads ἀπὸ | τῆς μείζονος. The collators' note on V, however, says 'Bas. image: τοῦ (compendium)'. P (the parallel, as reported by Vincent) also had τοῦ, and Vincent corrected it to τῆς. Варианты: If Bas. in fact prints τοῦ, add the entry 'τῆς H Par. V] τοῦ Bas.' and a departure. The adopted text τῆς (agreeing with εὐθεῖαν) does not change either way.
- Bas. 25.17: Claude's Bas. notes describe a printed «ἃλλα» (a λλ-compendium with rough breathing and grave) in line 25.17, but the established line «πρὸς τὸν πεντεκαιδέκατον ἔχει. καὶ τὰ μὲν» has no such word. Варианты: If Bas. really reads καὶ τὰ μὲν ἄλλα περὶ φθόγγων, then Bas. stands alone against H Par. V (καὶ τὰ μὲν περὶ φθόγγων) and is to be rejected: lemma 'μὲν H Par. V] μὲν ἄλλα Bas.'.

### Чтения для переводчика

- Bas. 25.24: в тексте «ὡς εἶναι τὸ ἡμιτόνιον ὅσον ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ», иначе «ὡς εἶναι τὸ ἡμιτόνιον οἷον ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ» (Bas. Par.). οἷον ('such as, as it were eight and a half') also gives good sense, as an illustration rather than a measure; it would soften a numerical statement that the author goes on to justify (26.1-2). It is the reading of both printed editions, and so of any translation made from them. The variatio οἷον … ὅσον is the less expected text, and levelling to ὅσον … ὅσον would be an easy change; H V and the quantitative sense favour ὅσον.
- Bas. 25.24-25: в тексте «ὅσον ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ … ὅσον ὀκτὼ τέταρτον: 'eight and a half' and 'eight and a quarter', i.e. with the tone as 9 : 8 the semitone reaches the point 8½ and the diesis the point 8¼ (counted from 8, 8½ : 8 = 17 : 16 and 8¼ : 8 = 33 : 32; the text gives only the numbers, not these ratios); the unit 9 − 8 is halved and quartered, which is why 26.1-2 adds that the unit is not divided in numbers but is divided in matter», иначе «ὅσον τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ … ὅσον τοῦ ἐννέα πρὸς ὀκτὼ τέταρτον: 'half of the ratio 9 : 8 … a quarter of the ratio 9 : 8'» (Xyl. (marginal supplements, no witness)). Xylander supplied these words in his margin, so a translation made from his text with its supplements will say 'half / a quarter of the ratio 9 : 8'. The translator should know both ways of understanding ὀκτὼ ἥμισυ. The transmitted text (all witnesses) means 8½ and 8¼, not 'half of 8'.
- Bas. 26.4: в тексте «τὴν γὰρ ἐπόγδοον διασώζουσαν λόγον νευρὰν: 'the string that keeps the epogdoic ratio (9 : 8)'», иначе «τὴν γὰρ ἐπόγδοον διασώζουσαν λόγον μοῖραν: 'the portion (segment) that keeps the epogdoic ratio'» (H). This is the reading of the oldest witness, and it makes good sense: the segment of the string corresponding to the tone is what is 'cut' into a semitone and then a diesis. Rejected mainly because the reading in H is uncertain and its accent points to νευράν.
- Bas. 25.4: в тексте «τὰς προσηγορίας λαμβάνοντες (nominative participle ad sensum: 'the notes, which take their names…')», иначе «τὰς προσηγορίας λαμβάνου(σι) (finite verb: '(the notes) take their names…')» (H s.l.? (interlinear, reading uncertain)). It may be a correction or variant in H. If the translator renders the participle as a finite clause, the result is the same.

### Сомнения

- P (Vinc. 338.4 and 338.7): P's σύμφρασις, twice, against the main text's συμφθάρσεως (27.10). The editor adopted the conjecture σύμφθαρσις / τὴν σύμφθαρσιν. Варианты: (a) σύμφθαρσις (adopted, the editor's conjecture). (b) σύμφρασις P, which would need a forced sense such as 'joining together into continuity'; if the human editor does not wish to emend P, print †σύμφρασις† or σύμφρασις with a note. (c) σύγκρασις, Vincent's suggestion in a note, which he did not print.
- P (Vinc. 338.7): ἐντεῦθεν ἐγγίνεται καὶ … εἶναι: P has the nominative ἡ σύμφρασις; Vincent printed the accusative τὴν σύμφρασιν. Варианты: (a) τὴν σύμφθαρσιν (adopted): impersonal ἐγγίνεται with accusative and infinitive. (b) ἡ σύμφθαρσις (P's case): ἐγγίνεται used personally, as in the first clause (τοῖς ἀριθμοῖς σύμφθαρσις οὐκ ἐγγίνεται), with a redundant εἶναι, possibly the author's own Byzantine construction.
- P (Vinc. 338.17): The numeral form for 16: P has ἑνδ(έκατον) (Vincent: 'Ms. ἑνδ.'), which is impossible arithmetically; Vincent printed ἑξδέκατον. Варианты: (a) ἑξκαιδέκατον (adopted, the editor's conjecture; an attested form, LSJ s.v. ἑξκαιδέκατος). (b) ἑκκαιδέκατον (classical). (c) ἑξδέκατον (Vinc.; the smallest change from P, but without an LSJ entry). (d) A cardinal ἑκκαίδεκα, perhaps from a numeral ιϛʹ misread as ιαʹ, parallel to τὰ ὀκτώ and τὸν τριακονταδύο ἀριθμόν in the same sentence.
- P (Vinc. 338.14): ὅπερ καὶ ἀριθμητικὸν ἀνεγχώρητον, 'which is impossible arithmetically', is awkward; kept as printed by Vincent without a note. Варианты: Keep ἀριθμητικόν (P). ### Чтения для переводчика

- Bas. 28.11–12 (and 28.6–7): в тексте «δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων … δὶς διὰ πέντε (literally 'twice through four', 'twice through five')», иначе «διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ τεσσάρων … διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ πέντε (octave plus fourth, 8:3; octave plus fifth, 3:1)» (Xyl. in marg. at 28.11–12 only; Bas. Par. H V (and P fr. 2) have δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων / δὶς διὰ πέντε). Kept as the author's own nomenclature: it is unanimous, it recurs at 28.6–7 and it appears in P fr. 2. By sense, however, the author means the fourth and fifth repeated over the octave. The context says that from the octachord the intervals are doubled again (κἀκεῖθεν αὖθις ἐπαναδιπλουμένων, 28.4), and these intervals are classed as consonant κατὰ ἀντίφωνον with the octave and the double octave. A literal double fourth (16:9) or double fifth (9:4) is not a consonance in any Greek theorist. Theon of Smyrna has κατ' ἀντίφωνον only for the octave and double octave, κατὰ παράφωνον for the fifth and fourth (Expos. p. 48.17–20 Hiller, Thrasyllus); the octave added to another consonance gives διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ τεσσάρων, διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ πέντε and δὶς διὰ πασῶν (p. 52.1–7, Adrastus); and the names are taken from the octachord lyre (p. 51.4–20). These parallels explain Xylander's note but do not decide the reading. Render so as not to suggest multiplication, e.g. 'the fourth again' / 'the fifth again' (in the second octave), as at 29.8–15, and add a note giving the sense 'octave plus fourth / fifth' (8:3, 3:1).
- Bas. 28.1: в тексте «ὡς δι’ ὀκτὼ τελειούμενον ('brought to completion / perfected through eight')», иначе «ὡς δι’ ὀκτὼ τελούμενον ('completed through eight')» (Bas. alone (against H Par. V)). The difference in sense is slight. τελειούμενον may carry a nuance of the octave as the 'perfect' or complete system, whereas the fifth at 27.25 is only τελούμενον. The translator may mark the difference or level it.
- P (Vinc. 338.4, 338.7): в тексте «σύμφθαρσις ('fusion', literally 'joint destruction', the merging of component intervals into one)», иначе «σύμφρασις P ('joining together, continuity'); σύγκρασις Vinc. ('blending')» (P as reported by Vincent; Vincent's note). If the human editor keeps P's word, the translation should still convey the merging of several intervals into one continuous interval. The argument (continuity of matter against the discreteness of number) is the same under all three readings.

### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 28.15: ἡπίως πρῶτον καὶ εὐρύθμως → ἠπίως πως καὶ εὐρύθμως (H Par. V). Bas. stands alone against H Par. V. The witnesses write the enclitic with an accent (πῶς); ἠπίως πως, 'in a gentle way, as it were', qualifies διαδεχομένων naturally. πρῶτον ('first') has no witness and no counterpart in the description of the antiphonon that follows. The correction of the breathing (ἡπίως, a misprint) is orthographic only.
- Bas. 29.13-14: τοῦ δὲ διὰ πασῶν → τοῦ διὰ πασῶν (H Par. V). Bas. alone has δὲ. In H Par. V the last two items of the list of ratios are asyndetic (τοῦ … διπλασιεπιμερής· τοῦ … τριπλάσιος), and no witness, Bas. included, has δὲ at the next item (29.15). Bas.'s δὲ looks like a regularisation by the Basel edition.
- Bas. 29.14: καὶ διὰ τεσσάρων → καὶ δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων (H V (Par. agrees with Bas.)). δὶς is in the whole tradition at all four places where the compound consonances are named: H and V at 29.8, 29.9, 29.14 and 29.15, Par. at three of them, and Bas. itself at 29.8-9. Bas. is inconsistent: it keeps δὶς at 29.8-9 with Xylander's marginal 'abundant omnino, et delenda sunt' and lacks it at 29.14-15, presumably by Xylander's silent correction, which here coincides with Par. The same usage is in P's addition (τὸ δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων, τὸ δὶς διὰ πέντε as the fourth and fifth above the octave, referred to the first term like 16:6 and 18:6). So it is the tradition's own, non-classical name for the fourth and fifth above the octave (classical: διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ τεσσάρων, Eucl. Sect. can. 12, Ptol. Harm. 1.5-6). By the editorial principle it is not to be classicised. Whatever is printed, the figures (8½ tones; 8:3 = 16:6) show that octave + fourth is meant. See the alternatives for translation.
- Bas. 29.15: καὶ διὰ πέντε → καὶ δὶς διὰ πέντε (H Par. V). Bas. stands alone. The tradition is consistent, as at 29.14: δὶς here belongs to the same usage as δὶς in 29.8-9 (Bas. included) and in P's addition. The figures (9½ tones at 29.10; 3:1 = 18:6) show that octave + fifth is meant.
- Bas. 29.21: τὸ ἕκτον → τὸν ἕκτον (H V (Par. agrees with Bas.)). In H, τὸ(ν) has the suspension stroke; V has τὸν. The masculine agrees with ὁ ἕκτος ἡμῖν ἀριθμός (29.17-18) and with the masculine ordinals used as numbers throughout: 16 'has the six twice and two parts of it' (12 + 4). The neuter τὸ ἕκτον would naturally mean 'the sixth part', which is wrong here. Bas. Par. probably derive from a copy whose abbreviation stroke was not read.
- Bas. 29.23 (after τέταρτος): — (passage absent; also absent from Par. H V) → ⟨⟨Σκοπητέον δὲ ὡς τὰ μὲν οὖν ἔμμεσα … ὅτι μηδὲ τοῖς ἀριθμητικοῖς τοῦτο δέδοκται.⟩⟩ (P (Vinc. 340.7–342.4), with Vincent's corrections and two conjectures of this edition inside the passage (προσλαμβανόμενον; ἐκ συμφθάρσεως, twice; see apparatus)). P's fragment 2. Vincent places it after τέταρτος (Par. 24v.20, the 3rd line from the bottom). It is absent at this place from all the other witnesses, so it is printed in ⟨⟨ ⟩⟩ as a P-only passage. Its internal text was established from Vincent's print and his footnotes; see the apparatus and p_treatment.
- Bas. 29.24: οὐ (printed οὒ, ου-ligature with breathing and grave) → ἢ (H Par. V P). Bas. stands alone against all the witnesses, and Vincent quotes the 1545 edition with ἢ. The sense is 'not by the number of the notes or of the strings … but by the tension'. The doubled negative οὐ … οὐ is unsupported and awkward. Compare the alternative 'of notes or strings' wording earlier in the treatise (εἴτ’ οὖν χορδῶν, Bas. p. 27, cited in the Bas. notes).

### Сомнения

- Bas. 29.15-16 (after τριπλάσιος): The list of ratios ends with the octave + fifth (τριπλάσιος), although the list of intervals just before it ends with the double octave (τὸ δὲ δὶς διὰ πασῶν διὰ δώδεκα, 29.10-11). The numerical example also goes on to τετραπλάσιος ὁ εἰκοστὸς τέταρτος (29.22-23), and P's addition counts the double octave among the intervals referred to the first term. A clause «τοῦ δὲ δὶς διὰ πασῶν τετραπλάσιος» may have fallen out by homoeoteleuton (τρι-πλάσιος … τετρα-πλάσιος). Варианты: (a) Keep as transmitted (adopted). (b) Mark a lacuna *** after τριπλάσιος. (c) Supply ⟨τοῦ δὲ δὶς διὰ πασῶν τετραπλάσιος⟩ (conjecture).
- Bas. 29.3-6, 29.11-23: Check of the harmonic content. The tone counts (fourth 2 tones + semitone + 'something small'; fifth 3 + semitone; octave 6; octave + fourth 8½; octave + fifth 9½; double octave 12) and the ratios and example numbers (6 : 8 : 9 : 12 : 16 : 18 : 24, giving 4:3, 3:2, 2:1, 8:3, 3:1, 4:1) agree in all witnesses and with each other. Two points are the author's own and are not to be emended. First, the fourth is said to exceed two tones and a semitone by an unnamed remainder smaller than a diesis, whereas Pythagorean theory makes it smaller than 2½ tones (Euclid, Sect. can. 15). Second, 8:3 is called by the generic name διπλασιεπιμερής (the specific name in Nicomachus' classification is διπλασιεπιδιμερής). Варианты: Keep everything as transmitted (adopted); the translator should note it rather than correct it.
- P (Vinc. 340.13-14 and 340.18): ἐξυφθάρσεως (Ms., twice) is not a known word, and Vincent's ἐξ φθάρσεως is neither grammatical (ἐξ before φ) nor meaningful in context ('intervals enlarged … of several intervals'; 'blended into one interval as by …'). Варианты: (a) †ἐξυφθάρσεως† (the editor's text). (b) ἐξ φθάρσεως (Vinc.). (c) ἐκ συνθέσεως (editor's conjecture: 'by composition of several intervals'; the passage is about composite intervals, and fragment 1 ends on ἀσύνθετα διαστήματα; ἐκ σ- may have been written ἐξ-). (d) ἐξ ὑφάνσεως (editor's conjecture: 'as by a weaving'; closer to the letters ἐξυφ-, and cf. πλέκονται in the same passage). (e) ἐκ συμφθάρσεως (reviewer's conjecture, printed by the arbiter's decision: 'the intervals enlarged by the fusion of several intervals'; 'are blended into one interval as by fusion'). LSJ s.v. σύμφθαρσις: the joint destruction of the components of a mixture when a new body is produced from them (Alex. Aphr. Mixt. 216.24), and the 'melting into one another' of things (Hermog. Id. 1.12; cf. Iambl. in Nic. p. 80 Pistelli). This is the sense required by κίρνανται and εἰς ἓν διάστημα.
- P (Vinc. 340.8): In «τὸ διὰ πέντε μέλος» the word μέλος is out of place in a list of interval names. It may be a corruption (of an abbreviation, a connective, or a gloss). Варианты: Keep as printed by Vinc. (adopted); possibly delete {μέλος} or obelize.
- P (Vinc. 340.9-10): τὸν προλαμβανόμενον φθόγγον is printed by Vincent without note. The technical name of the first (lowest) note of the Greater Perfect System is προσλαμβανόμενος; as far as I know, προλαμβανόμενος ('taken first') is not a note name. Варианты: Keep προλαμβανόμενον (P as printed by Vinc.), 'the note taken first', i.e. the base term (cf. 29.17 κείσθω πρῶτος ἐν ἀριθμοῖς ὅρος, the number 6); or correct to προσλαμβανόμενον (one letter; conjecture, printed by the arbiter's decision).
- P (Vinc. 340.11): Vincent prints [ἡ] δίεσις without a footnote; the meaning of his brackets (supplement or deletion) is not certain. Варианты: ⟨ἡ⟩ (adopted, treating it as Vincent's supplement); ἡ (if P has it); no article.
- P (Vinc. 340.20): In «καθ’ ὑπερβατὸν δὲ οἱ τῶν ἐν μέσῳ» the phrase must denote the composite intervals (ἔμμεσα), as the resumption shows: τοῦ μὲν τῶν ἐμμέσων διαστημάτων λόγου καθ’ ὑπερβατὸν (340.25-26), against κατὰ σειρὰν … τῶν ἀμέσων. Taken by itself, however, 'the intervals in the middle' would rather fit the ἄμεσα, which the same passage describes as μεταξὺ τούτων κείμενα (340.11). Варианты: (a) Keep οἱ τῶν ἐν μέσῳ as printed (adopted), understood as 'those with notes in the middle', a paraphrase of ἔμμεσα. (b) Correct to οἱ τῶν ἐμμέσων (reviewer's conjecture), as at 340.25 and in the opposition ἔμμεσα / ἄμεσα of 340.7-11.
- P (Vinc. 342.1-2): In «τοῦ δὲ τῶν ἀμέσων … συγκρινομένων» the participle is plural, while τοῦ (sc. λόγου) and the parallel παραβαλλομένου call for συγκρινομένου. Варианты: Keep συγκρινομένων (adopted; attracted to τῶν ἀμέσων, or a genitive absolute with τῶν ἀμέσων as subject); or correct to συγκρινομένου.

### Чтения для переводчика

- Bas. 29.8, 29.9, 29.14, 29.15: в тексте «τὸ διὰ πασῶν καὶ δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων / τὸ διὰ πασῶν καὶ δὶς διὰ πέντε», иначе «τὸ διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ τεσσάρων / τὸ διὰ πασῶν καὶ διὰ πέντε (δὶς deleted)» (Xyl. (marg. at 29.8-9: 'abundant omnino, et delenda sunt'); Bas. at 29.14-15; Par. at 29.14). Whichever reading is printed, the intervals meant are the octave plus a fourth (8½ tones; 8:3, exemplified 16:6) and the octave plus a fifth (9½ tones; 3:1, exemplified 18:6). They are not 'the octave and twice the fourth/fifth', which would be 11 and 13 tones (32:9 and 9:2). The translator should render δὶς here as marking the fourth/fifth 'again', in the second octave (as in P's τὸ δὶς διὰ τεσσάρων), with a note, and not as a multiplier. Xylander's text without δὶς gives the classical names.
- Bas. 28.15: в тексте «ἠπίως πως καὶ εὐρύθμως ('in a gentle, as it were, and rhythmical way')», иначе «ἠπίως πρῶτον καὶ εὐρύθμως ('first gently and rhythmically')» (Bas. (alone)). This is the Basel reading, which translations made from Bas. will follow; it is weaker but not absurd.
- P (Vinc. 340.9-10): в тексте «πρὸς τὸν προσλαμβανόμενον φθόγγον ('to the proslambanomenos', the lowest note of the system; conjecture)», иначе «πρὸς τὸν προλαμβανόμενον φθόγγον ('to the note taken first', i.e. the base term of the example)» (P (as printed by Vinc.)). Either way the same note is meant, the 1st of the fifteen notes counted in Bas. 28.19-22 and the base term 6 of the example. If the human editor keeps P's form, the translator may still name the note and give the technical term in a note.
- P (Vinc. 340.16): в тексте «τῶν ἐν αὐτοῖς κεκρυμμένων διαστημάτων ('the intervals contained, latent, in them')», иначе «τῶν ἐν αὐτοῖς κεκραμένων διαστημάτων ('the intervals mixed in them')» (Vinc. (P κεκρυμμένων)). Vincent's correction echoes κίρνανται ('are blended') at the end of the sentence. Both give good sense.
- P (Vinc. 340.13-14, 340.18): в тексте «ἐκ συμφθάρσεως ('by the fusion of several intervals' / 'as by fusion'; conjecture)», иначе «†ἐξυφθάρσεως† (P; untranslatable as it stands); ἐξ φθάρσεως ('from dissolution', Vinc.); or conj. ἐκ συνθέσεως ('by the composition of several intervals' / 'as by composition')» (P; Vinc.; editor's conjecture (ἐκ συνθέσεως)). ἐκ συμφθάρσεως is the author's own phrase (27.10, 32.10–11, 32.17) and is printed. If the human editor restores the obeli, the context (tridiesis, trihemitone, ditone, tritone as intervals made up of several smaller ones and 'blended into one') still gives the sense 'fusion' or 'composition'.
- P (Vinc. 342.4): в тексте «ὅτι μηδὲ τοῖς ἀριθμητικοῖς τοῦτο δέδοκται ('since not even the arithmeticians accept this')», иначе «ὅ, τι μηδὲ τοῖς ἀριθμητικοῖς οὔ πω δέδοκται ('a thing that not even the arithmeticians have yet accepted')» (Vinc. (P ὅτι … τοῦτο)). Vincent's printed text, which readers of Vincent will know. The sense is nearly the same.

### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 30.5: πρὸς τὸν πρὸ αὐτοῦ → πρὸς τοὺς πρὸ αὐτοῦ (H Par. (V τοὺς, but om. πρὸ)). Bas. stands alone against H Par. V. The plural is needed: the fifth note is then compared with each of the four notes before it (30.7-11: first, second, third, fourth), not with one.
- Bas. 30.23 (after ἀνέσεως): — (no text) → ⟨⟨Ἰστέον δὲ ὡς … εἶδος συνίστησι.⟩⟩ (P (Vinc. 342.19–24), with Vincent's correction ἐξαρτήσεως and one crux (†ἀμφότερον†) inside the passage (see apparatus)). A passage found only in P, at this place (Vinc.: it is wholly missing from the edition, which goes straight from ἀνέσεως to τῶν δὲ διαστ.). Printed in ⟨⟨ ⟩⟩ under the edition's rules. Its matter is a summary of Adrastus in Theon of Smyrna p. 65.10-66.11 Hiller (more weight gives the higher note; greater length and thickness of strings, and length and width of the holes of pipes, give the lower), so it is most probably a learned reader's addition, not the author's text. Within it ἐξαρτήσεως is Vinc.'s correction of P's ἐξαντήσεων (the word is in Theon p. 57.4 and 65.13 H.), and ἀμφότερον is obelised (see doubts).
- Bas. 30.23: τῶν δὲ διαστημάτων → τῶν δὲ συστημάτων (P (Vinc. 342.25–26), against Bas. H Par. V). Bas. Par. H V have διαστημάτων; P has συστημάτων, and Vincent already called the edition's reading a 'leçon fautive'. The author's own terminology decides. He defines a system as a compass of intervals and harmonia as the arrangement of systems (24.17–19). The very next sentence, in all witnesses, sets the systems against the intervals within them (Διαφέρει δὲ τὰ συστήματα τῶν ἐν μέσῳ διαστημάτων, 31.10), and he goes on to call the tetrachord and its successors systems (τὰ τοιαῦτα συστήματα, 31.13–14; τὰ λοιπὰ τῶν συστημάτων, 32.21–22, after the genera of the tetrachord; τῶν ἑξῆς συστημάτων, 33.1). The list itself keeps system and interval apart: the tetrachord holds the δυνάμεις of the fourth, the octachord the power of the octave, the sixteen-chord the διάστασις of the double octave. With διαστημάτων the author would call the tetrachord an interval and in the next sentence oppose systems to intervals. The corruption is easy: assimilation to τῶν δὲ διαστημάτων at the head of the paragraph on the intervals (25.19) and to τῶν διαστάσεων (30.16). The agreement of Bas. H Par. V weighs little here, since their common archetype errs two lines below (om. διὰ, 31.1). P's text of this passage is revised elsewhere (30.21–23 and the added paragraph), so συστημάτων may be a learned correction rather than an inherited reading (the reviewer compares Theon of Smyrna p. 49.14–17 Hiller, where tetrachords, pentachords and octachords are called systems); even so it restores the author's usage. Arbiter's decision on the reviewer's proposal; for the human editor's confirmation.
- Bas. 30.25: ἐμπεριέχων → ἐμπεριέχον (H Par. V). The neuter is required by τὸ … τετράχορδον, as with the parallel participles περιλαμβάνον, περιέχον and συνέχον. Bas. stands alone, probably a misprint (o/ω).
- Bas. 31.1: τὰς τοῦ † πέντε δυνάμεις → τὰς τοῦ ⟨διὰ⟩ πέντε δυνάμεις (Xyl. (margin: «† διὰ πέντε»); conjecture, no witness). The term is always διὰ πέντε: compare τοῦ διὰ τεσσάρων (30.24), τοῦ διὰ πασῶν (31.3), τοῦ δὶς διὰ πέντε (31.8). Bas. Par. H V all lack διὰ, so the omission is archetypal. It is supplied in ⟨ ⟩ because no witness has it.
- Bas. 31.2: τὴν * ὕλην → τὴν ὅλην (H (confirming Xyl.'s margin «* ὅλην»)). ὕλην does not construe next to the object ἰσχύν. ὅλην ('the whole power of the octave') matches ἁπάσας (30.25) and ὁλοσχερῆ (31.5). ὕλην is an error shared by Par. and V and carried into Bas.
- Bas. 31.5: διάτασιν → διάστασιν (H V). This is the author's word for an interval or extent (τῶν διαστάσεων λόγοι, 30.16, in all witnesses). H agrees with V, and V is Par.'s closest relative in this section (they share the error ὕλην, 31.2), so διάτασιν looks like an innovation of the printed tradition (Par., followed by Bas.).

### Сомнения

- Bas. 30.23: Decided by the arbiter: τῶν δὲ συστημάτων (P) is printed against τῶν δὲ διαστημάτων (Bas. Par. H V); see departures. Kept here as a record, because the text follows P against all four main witnesses. The tetrachord, pentachord, octachord and sixteen-chord are, in strict theory and in the author's own definitions, systems, not intervals. Варианты: (a) διαστημάτων (Bas. Par. H V; the editor's choice); (b) συστημάτων (P; the reviewer's proposal, printed).
- Bas. 30.23 (P passage): In P's added paragraph, τὸ πλεονάζον τούτων τινὸς ἀμφότερον τῆς μελῳδίας εἶδος συνίστησι gives no sense. The argument needs 'the opposite (low) kind of melody', in contrast to τὴν ἐπὶ ὀξὺ μελῳδίαν of the first member: more thickness or length of string or pipe, or more distance of the holes, lowers the pitch. Варианты: Print †ἀμφότερον† (done). My own conjectures, none supported by a checked parallel: θάτερον ('the other kind', i.e. the low); βαρύτερον; ἀμβλύτερον (graphically closest to ἀμφότερον). A lacuna is a weaker possibility (τὸ πλεονάζον ⟨καὶ τὸ ἐλλεῖπον⟩ … ἀμφότερον, 'excess and deficiency produce both kinds'), but it does not fit the antithesis with the first member. It may also be a slip in Vinc.'s transcription, which cannot be checked.
- Bas. 31.4: Number check on ἑκκαιδεκάχορδον. The double octave spans fifteen notes (2 × 8 − 1); the usual term for a fifteen-note system is πεντεκαιδεκάχορδον (LSJ s.v. cites Adrastus in Theon of Smyrna p. 64 H. and Ptolemy, Harmonics 3.1). Варианты: Keep ἑκκαιδεκάχορδον, the reading of all witnesses (adopted). Emendation to πεντεκαιδεκάχορδον is not justified.

### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 31.23: * ἢ ἁρμονία → ἡ ἁρμονία (H (68v.22) and Xylander's margin «* ἡ»). The subject of συνέχεται needs the article, as in the parallel clauses ἡ ἁρμονία προάγοιτο (31.19) and ἡ μελῳδία προάγοιτο (32.16). The disjunctive ἢ was suggested by the preceding μᾶλλον (μᾶλλον ἢ, 'rather than'). It leaves a comparison without a second member and makes ἁρμονία an anarthrous subject. Bas. Par. share ἢ. For V's reading see the doubts.
- Bas. 32.5–6: ἐκτιθε|μένων → ἐντιθε|μένων (H (69r.8–9), Par. (26r.21), V (81v.10)). Bas. stands alone against all the other witnesses. Bas. took the text over from the earlier edition, as Par. shows, and Par. has ἐν-, so ἐκ- is a misprint of Bas. or an unsupported change by Xylander. ἐντίθημι, 'insert, place within', describes the two tone-intervals placed inside the fourth exactly: once they are in, the remainder must be a semitone.
- Bas. 32.15: γοέτερον → γοερώτερον (H (69r.19)). γοέτερον is not a Greek form. The comparative of γοερός (short syllable before the ending) is regularly γοερώτερος, like σοφώτερος. LSJ s.v. γοερός gives no comparative (checked), so the form is a regular formation, not a recorded rarity. The syllable -ρω- was lost, perhaps under the influence of the familiar ending -έτερος (ἡμέτερος, ἑκάτερος). Par. and V share the error, so it is not a misprint of Bas. but an error of the branch the printed text comes from. The oldest witness keeps the correct form.

### Сомнения

- Bas. 31.23 (V 81v.1): The section file gives two different readings for V at this place. The doubt concerns the apparatus only: the constituted text (ἡ, from H and Xyl.) is the same either way. Варианты: (a) V reads ἢ, as in the established V line, i.e. with Bas. and Par.; (b) V reads ἡ, as Claude's collators' note on V says ('rough breathing, no grave … V agrees with Xylander').
- Bas. 32.11–13: How to construe and punctuate ἐξ ἡμιτονίων δύο καὶ τριημιτονίου συγκεκραμένον. The participle cannot qualify τριημιτόνιον, which cannot be made of itself plus two semitones. ### Отступления от Bas.

- Bas. 32.23: τὸ (τετραχόρδου), marked * by Xylander → τοῦ (τετραχόρδου) (H (69v.5 τοῦ τε|τραχόρδου); also Xyl.'s margin «* τοῦ»). τὸ cannot be construed. The phrase τῷ … ἑνὸς τοῦ τετραχόρδου … συντακτικῷ ὑποδείγματι needs the genitive article with τετραχόρδου, depending on ὑποδείγματι. Bas. Par. V share the error, which is probably a misread abbreviation of τοῦ. H and Xylander's correction agree.
- Bas. 33.12: ὑμεραῖος → ὑμέναιος (H Par. V). A Bas. misprint: the ερ-ligature was set for εν. The name of the wedding song (ὑμέναιος) is needed in the list παιήων, ὑμέναιος, ἐξόδιος. Bas. stands alone.
- Bas. 33.14: μόνῃ διαφορᾷ, ἀλλὰ → μόνῃ μεταφορᾷ, οὐδὲ τῇ μόνῃ τούτων διαφορᾷ, ἀλλὰ (H Par. V). Bas. stands alone against all three witnesses, including Par., the reprint of the editio princeps. Bas. depends on the printed tradition: it shares χολαιοτέραις (33.15) with Par. against H V, and τὸ (32.23) with Par. V against H. So the loss happened in Bas. itself. A skip from μόνῃ to μόνῃ would leave τούτων in place (μόνῃ τούτων διαφορᾷ), so that explanation needs a second loss. There is a one-step explanation in Par.'s layout: 27r.18 … μόνῃ με-| 27r.19 ταφορᾷ. οὐδὲ τῇ μόνῃ τούτων δια-| 27r.20 φορᾷ. ἀλλὰ. Here the words missing in Bas. are exactly those between the line-end syllable με- and the line-end syllable δια- directly below it. A compositor setting from a copy with this lineation could pass from one to the other and produce μόνῃ διαφορᾷ. It is not known whether the 1532 princeps had Par.'s lineation, and a deliberate shortening cannot be ruled out. The fuller text is the better sense. The two negative clauses match the two things just discussed. The shifting (μεταφορά) of the intervals gives the λύδιον/φρύγιον/δώριον melodies. The difference (διαφορά) of the intervals gives the genera, such as the enharmonic and the diatonic just discussed. ἀλλὰ καὶ then adds tempo, sustained notes, flow and instrumental technique.
- Bas. 33.15: χολαιοτέραις → σχολαιοτέραις (H V). χολαιοτέραις is not a word. σχολαιότερος ('slower, more leisurely') is the necessary partner of ταχιναῖς ('quick') with μεταβάσεσι. Bas. and Par. share the loss of σ (or the σχ-ligature was misread; see doubts).

### Сомнения

- Bas. 33.15: The established diplomatic line of Bas. reads χολαιοτέραις, and so does Par. Claude's own note on Bas. 33.15, however, describes the printed first sort as a σχ-ligature that 'resembles χ', with the reading σχολαιοτέραις. This changes only how Bas. is reported, not the constituted text, which is σχολαιοτέραις in either case. Варианты: (a) Bas. = χολαιοτέραις, as in the established line: the apparatus entry 'σχολαιοτέραις H V] χολαιοτέραις Bas. Par.' and the departure stand. (b) Bas. = σχολαιοτέραις with a σχ-ligature: the entry becomes 'σχολαιοτέραις Bas. H V] χολαιοτέραις Par.' and the departure is withdrawn.

### Чтения для переводчика

- Bas. 32.21: в тексте «τὸν ὅμοιον δὲ τρόπον … ἡ ἁρμονία συντάττει ('In the same way, harmonia arranges the remaining systems too')», иначе «τὸν ὅμοιον γὰρ τρόπον … ('For in the same way, harmonia arranges the remaining systems too')» (H (the oldest witness), alone). With γὰρ the sentence becomes a justification of what precedes: the enharmonic genus takes its name from ἁρμονία. That is possible, but the next sentence also begins with γὰρ, and δὲ gives the natural transition. For the translator: ἡ ἁρμονία here very probably means the enharmonic genus itself, its old name, as the preceding παρωνυμία-clause suggests, rather than 'harmonics' in general.
- Bas. 33.13–14: в тексте «οὐ γὰρ τῇ τῶν διαστημάτων μόνῃ μεταφορᾷ, οὐδὲ τῇ μόνῃ τούτων διαφορᾷ, ἀλλὰ καὶ … ('not only by the shifting of the intervals, nor only by their difference, but also by …')», иначе «οὐ γὰρ τῇ τῶν διαστημάτων μόνῃ διαφορᾷ, ἀλλὰ καὶ … ('not by the difference of the intervals alone, but also by …')» (Bas. alone (1556)). The shorter text of Bas. is coherent, and it is the text of the 1556 Basel edition. Any rendering or quotation made from Bas. will lack the μεταφορά-clause, so the translator should not be surprised by the difference. The adopted text separates the transposition or shifting of intervals (behind the Lydian, Phrygian and Dorian melodies) from their difference (the genera).
